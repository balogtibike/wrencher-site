Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

30-day money-back guarantee

We stand behind Wrencher. If you purchase a license and are not satisfied for any reason, we will refund your payment in full within 30 days of purchase — no questions asked.

How to request a refund

Email us at hello@wrencher.app with your order number (found in your purchase confirmation email) and the email address used to purchase.

We will process your refund within 2 business days. Refunds are returned to the original payment method. Depending on your bank, funds may take 3–5 business days to appear.

After a refund

Once a refund is issued, your license key will be deactivated. You are welcome to continue using the free trial for the remainder of its 30-day period.

Eligibility

Refunds are available for purchases made within the last 30 days. For annual licenses, refunds are available within 30 days of each annual payment.

Free trial

Wrencher offers a full 30-day free trial with no credit card required. We encourage you to try the software thoroughly before purchasing.

Contact

Questions? Email hello@wrencher.app — we respond within one business day.
