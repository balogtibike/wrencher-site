Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

The short version

Wrencher is a desktop application that runs entirely on your computer. Your business data — clients, jobs, quotes, invoices — is stored locally on your machine and never uploaded to any server. We collect very little about you, and we never sell your data.

1. Who we are

Wrencher is a software product. You can contact us at hello@wrencher.app.

2. What data the application stores

All application data is stored locally on your computer at %APPDATA%\Wrencher\ on Windows. This includes client names, addresses, phone numbers and email addresses you enter; job details, notes, and photos; quotes, invoices, and financial figures; mileage logs; your business settings and logo.

None of this data is transmitted to us or to any server. It remains entirely under your control.

3. What data we collect through this website

When you visit wrencher.app, standard web server logs may record your IP address, browser type, and pages visited for security and aggregate traffic analysis. We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising.

If you submit the contact form, your name, email address, and message are received by us via Web3Forms and used only to respond to your enquiry.

4. Payments

Payments are processed by Paddle (paddle.com). We never see or store your card number. We receive only your email address and order details from Paddle for the purpose of delivering your license key.

5. License activation

When you activate a license key, the app makes a one-time call to verify the key and record the activation. We store this to enforce the 2-computer activation limit and to allow license transfers.

6. Email communications

If you purchase a license, we will email you your license key. We may also email you about important updates. We do not send marketing emails or share your email address with third parties.

7. Third-party services

This website uses Google Fonts. No other third-party tracking or analytics services are used.

8. Your rights

You have the right to request a copy, correction, or deletion of any personal data we hold. Email hello@wrencher.app to exercise these rights.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.

10. Contact

Questions? Email hello@wrencher.app.
