Jobs

Job management for tradespeople — from enquiry to complete.

Keep every job in one place — plumbing callouts, electrical work, building projects, or landscaping. Set status, priority, dates, and address. Dashboard alerts tell you when something needs attention before it becomes a problem.

  • Status tracking: Enquiry → Quoted → In Progress → Complete
  • Priority levels and job types (residential, commercial, emergency)
  • Start and end dates with overdue alerts
  • Photo attachments directly on the job
  • Notes and full address per job
  • Dashboard attention cards for jobs needing action
Calendar

See your week. Plan your schedule.

Week and month views show every job at a glance. Jobs without dates sit in an undated strip above the calendar until you're ready to schedule them.

  • Week and month calendar views
  • Job bars spanning start to end date
  • Undated jobs strip — schedule when ready
  • Reschedule by clicking and setting a new date
  • Colour-coded by job status
Quotes

Professional quoting software — send quotes in minutes.

Build quotes from your price book, generate a PDF instantly, and send straight to the client. Single quotes or multi-option A/B/C quotes — the quoting software tradespeople actually use without needing an accountant to explain it.

  • Single quotes or multi-option quotes (A/B/C pricing)
  • Price book — store your standard items and rates
  • Tax, discounts, and deposit support
  • Expiry dates with dashboard alerts
  • PDF generated instantly with your logo
  • Send by email directly from the app
  • Accepted quote converts to invoice in one click
Invoices

Invoicing software for tradespeople — get paid faster.

Create invoices from accepted quotes in one click, or build blank invoices for quick jobs. Set up recurring invoices for maintenance clients. Track every payment — outstanding, overdue, and paid — at a glance. No subscription invoicing software, just a one-time license.

  • Invoice from accepted quote in one click
  • Blank invoices for quick jobs
  • Recurring invoices — monthly, quarterly, annually
  • Partial payments tracked
  • Overdue alerts on dashboard
  • Professional PDF with your logo
  • Send payment reminders by email
Change orders

Charge for every change. Every time.

When scope changes mid-job, raise a change order. Get it approved. It rolls automatically into the final invoice — nothing falls through the cracks.

  • Raise change orders against any job
  • Pending, approved, and declined statuses
  • Dashboard alert for pending change orders
  • Approved items automatically included in invoice
  • Change order PDF for client approval
Mileage tracking

Mileage tracking for self-employed tradespeople.

Log every trip to site, link it to a job, and export a clean CSV mileage report for your accountant at the end of the tax year. Works in miles or kilometres — nothing missed, nothing forgotten.

  • Log trips with date, from, to, distance, and purpose
  • Link trips to jobs
  • Miles or kilometres
  • Configurable rate per mile/km
  • This month, this year, and all-time totals
  • CSV export for your accountant
Reports & statistics

Reports and statistics for your trade business.

Stop guessing which jobs are profitable and which clients are worth chasing. Wrencher gives sole traders and independent contractors the numbers they need to make real business decisions — revenue by month, outstanding invoices, quote conversion, and more.

  • Revenue by month — spot your busy and slow periods
  • Outstanding invoices — see every unpaid balance in one view
  • Job profitability — know which jobs earn and which don't
  • Client revenue ranking — focus on your best clients
  • Quote conversion rate — see how many quotes turn into jobs
  • Overdue tracking — never lose money to forgotten invoices again
